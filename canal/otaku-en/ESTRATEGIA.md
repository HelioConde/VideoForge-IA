# Editorial strategy — Otaku Reframe (Otaku EN)

> **Plano editorial, não execução.** Pesquisa de trends, vidIQ, geração de imagens, narração, Analytics e publicação são integrações futuras. Não criar candidatos, reservas, históricos ou métricas fictícios. Ler antes: `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/ARQUITETURA.md`, `docs/REGRAS-EDITORIAIS.md`, `docs/ESTRATEGIA_SHORTS.md`, `canal/README.md`, `canal/<id>/README.md` e histórico **do próprio canal**.

## Channel positioning

**English-language anime explainers, theories and original analyses**, illustrated with AI-generated visuals and narrated in natural English. This is an **independent channel**: never automatically translate/re-upload Otaku BR scripts, artwork or videos. Research English-language viewers separately and create distinct angles with added value.

- **Audience:** English-speaking anime viewers (initial research may compare United States, United Kingdom, Canada and other markets, but do not assume which countries the actual subscribers live in).
- **Editorial promise:** clear English anime insight, meaningful original commentary, satisfying short-form narrative, consistent illustrated aesthetics.
- **Initial experiments, not performance-proven proportions:** 30% character and lore explainers; 30% anime theories and “what if” concepts with clear speculation labels; 25% story/event breakdowns; 15% verified news about releases and adaptations.

## English-language trend research

Use English YouTube Search/Shorts for relevant markets, Google Trends filtered to **YouTube Search** and selected country, verifiable public competitor examples, licensing/distributor announcements (e.g. [Crunchyroll News](https://www.crunchyroll.com/news)), official production accounts and audience questions where comments are available. Compare recent periods and publication dates. When authorized and technically available, study the **Otaku EN** channel's own Analytics; never import Otaku BR's audience metrics as Otaku EN facts.

Do not assume viral clips are legitimate news or use unsourced trailers. Confirm release dates and factual claims via official sources. For spoilers, signal “spoilers” clearly in the packaging.

## Topic formats and working examples (not verified trends)

- “Why [character]'s decision changes everything in [anime]” — backed by story evidence.
- “The hidden rule behind [power system]” — differentiate canon from interpretation.
- “What if [event] never happened?” — explicitly hypothetical, written as an original argument.
- “What we actually know about [season/project]” — sourced official announcements, no invented release date.

Every video must have a distinct central question, context, original insight, beginning, development and payoff. Natural-English script review should catch machine-like phrasing, cultural mismatch, mispronounced names and unnatural SEO stuffing. Title, voice-over, subtitles, description and call-to-action must be **in English**, unless an intentional quoted term needs its original language.

## AI image storyboard and licensing

Maintain a single locked **anime illustration style bible** across frames: character proportions/identifiers, costumes, framing, lighting and backgrounds. Use AI drawings only where the subject can be represented truthfully; clearly distinguish original illustrative art from official anime footage or promotional key art. Do not recreate copyrighted frames, use copyrighted OST or imitate actors/voice performers without clearance. If referencing a copyrighted franchise or character, verify rights/permission as appropriate; fan art does not automatically grant commercial-use permission. Avoid realistic AI imagery implying an actual event or person unless disclosure requirements are met.

## Editorial scoring (each 0–5)

Validated demand in English-language markets **25%**; relevance to Otaku EN's actual audience **20%**; strength and clarity of original argument **20%**; distinctiveness from this channel's history and Otaku BR's output **15%**; feasibility with consistent AI imagery **10%**; factual verification/rights readiness **10%**. Any unsupported facts, misleading packaging, reused clone or rights problem is an automatic veto.

## Measurement and iteration

Compare topics and hooks using Otaku EN's **own** engaged views, viewed-versus-swiped, average percentage viewed, audience retention and subscribers per 1,000 engaged views when measurable. Segment by market and language only when reliable real metrics exist. Use controlled tests of explainer vs theory, direct reveal vs question hook and title styles. Do not generalize the winning format from Otaku BR.

## Execução editorial e rastreabilidade (a implementar)

1. Conferir identidade, idioma, histórico e reservas **deste canal** e inventário real do YouTube quando conectado; índice vazio não prova ausência de vídeos.
2. Pesquisar várias ideias atuais com links e datas. Não chamar qualquer título de “em alta” sem evidências verificáveis.
3. Eliminar assuntos repetidos, histórias quase iguais, dados sem fonte, risco de direitos ou impossibilidade de representar o tema com imagens de IA.
4. Atribuir notas editoriais justificadas (0–5 em cada critério ponderado); as notas são **avaliação interna**, jamais números de Analytics ou “score do algoritmo”. Quando faltar evidência, marcar como pendente, sem simular resultados.
5. Registrar candidato realmente pesquisado em `editorial/pesquisas/YYYY-MM-DD.json` e, após seleção real, em `editorial/candidatos.json` / `reservas.json`. Usar `videos/<video-id>/editorial.json` para roteiro, palavras-chave, fontes, guia visual, direitos e aprovação; `video.json` continua exclusivamente técnico.
6. Criar roteiro com gancho imediato, progressão, desfecho e indicação de cada cena; produzir uma **bíblia visual** única por vídeo antes das imagens (estilo, personagens, roupa, cenário, cor, proporções, luz). Usar a mesma base de referência nas cenas, revisar consistência e corrigir imagens discrepantes. Imagens devem ser pertinentes ao que é narrado, não um slideshow genérico.
7. Fazer QA editorial (originalidade, idade, precisão, direitos, legibilidade, coerência visual), além de QA técnico do MP4. Não afirmar produção, upload ou desempenho sem artefatos reais.
8. Quando houver publicação autorizada, acompanhar resultados por vídeo no histórico do **mesmo canal** e revisar temas, ganchos, formatos e frequência a partir dos dados reais; não há garantia de viralização nem obrigação de postar diariamente.

**Fontes transversais:** [YouTube Creators](https://www.youtube.com/intl/pt-BR/creators/), [descoberta de Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=pt-BR), [até 3 minutos e copyright](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=pt-BR) e [conteúdo inautêntico](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=pt-BR). ## 30-day Shorts experiment — research diagnosis (2026-11-13)

**Data provenance:** the deep-research report based on YouTube Studio exports for **2026-10-16 through 2026-11-13** reports **3,552 views** and **7 subscribers gained** for Otaku Reframe. These figures have **not been independently recalculated from the source CSVs** in this repository; they are not live Analytics.

**Editorial hypothesis:** prioritize **anime dilemmas, meaningful choices and explanations of rules/power systems**. Deliver a clear premise in the opening 1–3 seconds and a sourced, satisfying payoff. Treat this as a channel-specific direction, not a reason to translate Otaku BR output or borrow its performance data. Scripts, narration, captions, titles and descriptions must be idiomatic English.

**Optional 30-day allocation:** up to **10 independently researched candidate Shorts**: 4 character or story dilemmas, 3 canon-backed rule explainers, 2 distinct original analyses and 1 verified release/update. This mix is an **operational proposal**, not a transcription of the report's ten detailed ideas, and does not override editorial vetoes. Validate each angle against Otaku EN history and public sources before creating a candidate or reservation.

**Measurement if authorized:** viewed versus swiped, engaged views, average view duration/percentage viewed, subscriber gains and market-specific evidence **from Otaku EN only**. Compare dilemma and rule formats while recording subject, opening style, length and language quality; avoid declaring a winner from one video. No automatic reuse of Brazilian scripts, assets, metadata or footage.

**Production guardrails:** verify canon versus speculation, spoilers and rights; maintain visual continuity and human-quality English. Nothing in this plan enables the currently unimplemented channel renderer, AI agents or YouTube publishing.
